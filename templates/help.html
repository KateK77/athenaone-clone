Follow a visit from start to finish

  1. Front desk: Search Patients before registration. Add insurance, verify eligibility, schedule a weekday visit, confirm it, then mark Arrived. Complete the check-in checklist.
  2. Nurse / assistant: Open the encounter from the clinical inbox. Record intake and vitals, complete medication/allergy/history review, then move the appointment to Roomed.
  3. Physician: Move the appointment to In Progress. Open the encounter, add a diagnosis, document SOAP sections, place and sign orders. Review any AI draft before applying it. Save, then sign the note.
  4. Lab workflow: Move signed orders to Processing, enter a result, publish it. A physician reviews the result and explicitly releases it to the portal.
  5. Coder: Open a signed encounter, add procedure charges and approve them. Generate a claim.
  6. Billing: Review, validate, submit, receive simulated payer response, and adjudicate. The selected deterministic scenario controls the outcome. Record an appeal for a denial, correct it, then revalidate and resubmit.
  7. Patient: View released results, signed summaries, appointments and medications. Request a visit or renewal, send a message, or make a simulated payment after adjudication.

Demonstration boundaries

All data is fictional. No pharmacy, payer, payment, or clinical network is connected. AI mode is a deterministic text organizer, not validated clinical decision support. Terminology catalogs are small curated examples. JSON exports are FHIR-inspired and have not been validated for formal conformance.

Keyboard & accessibility

Press / to focus patient search. Use Tab to navigate, Enter to activate actions, and Escape to close the note preview. Forms preserve invalid values and display validation errors.
